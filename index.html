Our Mission

Buy. Renovate. Revitalize.

We target Qualified Census Tracts, Opportunity Zones, and Community Reinvestment Act areas — places where capital has been scarce and housing stock has declined. Our cluster strategy (8–15 contiguous properties) creates visible neighborhood transformation rather than scattered, isolated flips.

🏘️

Cluster Strategy

8–15 contiguous parcels per micro-zone. Visible transformation on the same streets creates community momentum and shared pride of ownership.

💰

Affordable Resale

Every home sold affordably to a workforce household (60–120% AMI). Not flips — workforce housing that families can actually buy.

📊

Disciplined Underwriting

Total basis ≤ 75% of new-build replacement cost. Construction LTC ≤ 80%. Every deal scored against our 100-point Arcstead scorecard before a dollar moves.

🔒

Human-Gated Commitments

Automation prepares the pipeline. Humans make every commitment. No offer, contact, or capital deployment happens without explicit Nathan / CEO approval.
