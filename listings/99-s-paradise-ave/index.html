A 1941 brick house on nearly half an acre, close to downtown Catonsville, with something you rarely find in a home this age: an elevator.

Houses from this era were built to be lived in for generations. This one still has the arched doorways, the built-ins and the hardwood floors, and it sits on a 0.44-acre corner lot with mature trees and a yard that is fenced front and back.

The current owners have put about $50,000 into it, including the kitchen, a bathroom, the flooring and a new patio. The finished lower level adds a full bath and a second kitchen. It is not counted as a bedroom, and the price reflects the room that level still has to improve.
